How much does a Kenya safari cost? Fair question. 💰
Typical prices per person per day: US$250–400 in comfortable camps, US$450–750 in mid-range and luxury tented camps, and US$800–1,500+ at the top end. Most cover your stay, meals, park fees and game drives.
Travel in April or May, or share the vehicle, and it drops. 😉
