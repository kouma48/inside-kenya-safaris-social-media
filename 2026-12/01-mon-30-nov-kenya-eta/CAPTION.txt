Passport? Check. eTA? Let's make sure. ✈️
Kenya replaced its eVisa with the Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) in 2024. Apply only on the official site, etakenya.go.ke, two to three weeks before you fly. Your passport needs six months' validity and two blank pages.
New since October: most visitors also add Kenya travel health insurance when they apply.
