Red elephants. Yes, really. 🐘
Tsavo's elephants dust-bathe in iron-rich red soil, and it stains their skin a vivid ochre. The park is Kenya's largest, at over 20,000 km², with 10,000+ elephants and the clear pools of Mzima Springs.
Best of all? Some days you won't see another vehicle for an hour. 😉
