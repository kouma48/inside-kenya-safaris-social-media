The one packing rule that catches people out? Soft bags. 🎒

Small safari flights in Kenya typically limit you to about 15 kg per person. So pack light, soft and neutral: layers in khaki, olive or beige, a brimmed hat, and binoculars before anything else. Leave the hard suitcase, heavy jewellery and strong perfume at home.
