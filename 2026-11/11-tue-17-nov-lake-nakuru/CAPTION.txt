A fenced rhino sanctuary, a flamingo lake and Rothschild's giraffe. All a few hours from Nairobi. 🦏

Lake Nakuru sits in the floor of the Great Rift Valley, ringed by acacia woodland and rocky escarpments. Its sanctuary holds black and white rhino, so a sighting is far more reliable than in most open parks. The flamingos move between soda lakes with the water, but the birdlife is outstanding.

Give it one or two nights. Link in bio.
