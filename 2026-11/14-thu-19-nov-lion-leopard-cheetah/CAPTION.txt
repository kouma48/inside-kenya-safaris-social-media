Lion, leopard or cheetah? Kenya says: why not all three? 🐆
Lions live in family groups and are the easiest to find. Leopards are solo, nocturnal and shy. Cheetahs need open plains and short grass to sprint.
Our tip: the Maasai Mara is the most realistic place to see all three. Give it four to five nights, with morning and evening drives. 😉
