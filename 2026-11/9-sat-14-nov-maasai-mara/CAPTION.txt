Everyone talks about the crossing. The Mara has more to show you.

From July to October, two million wildebeest, zebra and gazelle move through, with the river crossings at their most dramatic in August and September. But the plains reward every month: short grass and wide horizons, some of the highest big-cat densities in Africa, and lion, leopard and cheetah in view all year.

Our advice after more than 30 years guiding the Mara: choose a private conservancy for a first visit. Fewer vehicles at every sighting, plus walking safaris and night drives.
