Everyone talks about the crossing. The Mara has so much more.

July to October brings two million wildebeest, zebra and gazelle. But the plains reward every month, with lion, leopard and cheetah in view all year.

Our tip after 30+ years guiding here: choose a private conservancy for a first visit. Fewer vehicles at every sighting.

Save this for your trip planning, and share it with someone heading on safari.
